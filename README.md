# My Favorite Series

## About the Series
One of my favorite series is **Breaking Bad**.  
It’s about a *high school chemistry teacher* who turns to making illegal drugs after being diagnosed with cancer.  
The story shows how his decisions slowly change his life and everyone around him.

## Why I Like It
- The character development is amazing.  
- The storytelling and tension keep you hooked.  
- It teaches lessons about choices and consequences.

## Favorite Character
**Walter White** – a brilliant yet tragic character who shows how good intentions can spiral out of control.

## Fun Fact
The blue crystal in the series is actually *blue rock candy* used as a prop.
